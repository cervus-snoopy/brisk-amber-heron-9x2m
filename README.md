## The lines

> Don't sell your red Polygon bike to the boy on Jalan Merdeka for twenty dollars on Saturday morning.
> The twenty dollars is gone by Monday, but the bike was the only way you ever got to school on time, and losing it is the first morning you stop going.

Two lines. The field is crowded with voicemails, 2 a.m. texts, contracts and night shifts — this one is the moment nobody wrote: selling your first bike for twenty dollars on a Saturday morning, and the twenty dollars gone by Monday while the bike was the only way you ever got to school on time.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes the two lines to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` — UTF-8, no trailing newline — and leaves the file readable by every user (0644, folders 0755).

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/output python3 main.py
```
